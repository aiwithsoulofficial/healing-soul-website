The Foundation

Healing that covers
all three dimensions

Most apps cover mind and body. Healing Soul adds the soul. Because to heal deeply, all three must be seen - and worked with.

01 First Dimension

Mind

Guided journal prompts that bring your thoughts, patterns, and mental blocks to the surface. Understand the why behind the wound - not just the what.

02 Second Dimension

Body

Somatic awareness and body-based releases. Breathwork, grounding, and movement tools that help your body process what your mind has opened up.

03 Third Dimension

Soul

The dimension most apps miss entirely. Lunar rituals, intention-setting, and practices that connect you to something deeper - integrating healing at a soul level.

What's Inside

Your complete
healing toolkit

Healing Soul is more than a journal. It's a living companion for your inner work - equipped with tools, releases, and guided check-ins to support you between sessions, on hard days, and in the quiet moments when something needs to move.

"There's a part of you that already knows what needs to heal. We're just here to help you listen."

Curated prompts across Mind, Body, and Soul. Light and deep modes for wherever you are today.

Breathwork + EFT

Lunar Rituals

Manifestation + Mindset

Daily Routines

Morning affirmations, mid-day check-ins, evening reflections. A healing practice built into your day.
